Privacy Policy

Keepy Uppy Job Tracker · Last updated November 21, 2026

Keepy Uppy Job Tracker does not collect, store, or share your personal information. The short version: your data stays on your phone, and in your own iCloud if you choose.

What the app stores

The jobs, services, clients, invoice details, and business information you enter are saved on your iPhone. We never receive them. We have no servers, no accounts, and no database of users.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your data in your own private iCloud account, so it is backed up and can appear on your other Apple devices. This copy belongs to you and is protected by your Apple ID. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can turn this off for the app in Settings, under your name, then iCloud.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.

Sharing

The app shares information only when you ask it to. When you send an invoice or export a CSV, the file goes where you choose, such as the Mail app, Messages, or Files. That is your decision and happens through the apps on your phone, not through us.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or notifications.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its data from that phone. If iCloud is on, your copy stays in your iCloud account until you delete it there, in Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage. There is nothing to delete on our side because we hold nothing.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the support page.
