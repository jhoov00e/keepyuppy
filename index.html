Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud account if you are signed in, so it is backed up and follows you to a new phone. There is no account with us and no server. Use Export on the Summary tab to save a CSV of any year.

Why won't invoices send by email? Sending by email needs the Mail app set up on your phone. If it isn't, Keepy Uppy opens the share sheet instead, so you can send the PDF with Messages or any other app.

How do I change my business name or invoice note? Tap the building icon on the Log tab (Business info).

How do I see the welcome tour again? Business info, then "Replay the welcome tour".

I deleted the app or got a new phone. Can I get my jobs back? Yes, if iCloud was on. Sign in to the same Apple ID, install Keepy Uppy, and your jobs, clients, and services come back. Without iCloud, only a CSV export can restore a record.
